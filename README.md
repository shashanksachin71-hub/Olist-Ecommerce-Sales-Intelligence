# Olist E-Commerce Sales Intelligence

## 📊 Project Overview

Olist E-Commerce Sales Intelligence is an end-to-end data analytics project built using the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Public Dataset.

The project analyzes sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, seller performance, payment methods, reviews, and delivery operations.

The goal is to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights using Python, SQL, RFM analysis, and Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Analyze seller and product performance
- Evaluate delivery performance
- Identify late-delivery patterns
- Analyze payment methods and customer reviews
- Build an interactive executive dashboard

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented using **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** analysis.

The major customer segments include:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- New Customers
- At Risk
- Need Attention
- Lost Customers

This segmentation helps identify valuable customers and customers who may require retention efforts.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Health & Beauty** was the highest-revenue product category.
- **São Paulo (SP)** generated the highest customer-state revenue.
- Average delivery time was approximately **12 days**.
- Approximately **6.77%** of delivered orders were classified as late.
- **Champions** and **Loyal Customers** represent important high-value customer segments.
- A significant portion of customers falls into retention-focused segments such as **At Risk**, **Need Attention**, and **Lost Customers**.
